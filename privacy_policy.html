Privacy Policy for Zikirmatik

Last updated: January 2025

1. Overview

Zikirmatik ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Data Collection

Zikirmatik stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Data stored locally includes:

  • Zikr counter values
  • Custom zikr names and counts
  • Goals and statistics
  • App settings and preferences

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Location Data

The app requests location permission solely to determine your country for automatic language selection. Location data is not stored or transmitted.

5. Data Export/Import

You can export your data to CSV/JSON files. These files remain under your control and are not sent to us or any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
